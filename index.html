05. Career Pathway: 2026–2030

Year 1 — Build Foundations

Strengthen academic fundamentals, learn Excel, practise basic statistics and become comfortable using a computer and digital tools.

Target: complete regular exercises and one beginner spreadsheet analysis.

Year 2 — Develop Technical Skills

Learn SQL and Python basics, practise with public datasets and study data cleaning and visualization.

Target: complete one guided course and one original mini-project.

Year 3 — Gain Practical Experience

Develop Power BI skills, create a portfolio, improve communication and apply for relevant internships.

Target: publish two well-documented projects and seek internship experience.

Year 4 — Prepare for Employment

Refine projects, prepare a targeted CV, practise interviews and apply for graduate roles.

Target: complete a capstone project and maintain a consistent job-application plan.

Internship and Placement Strategy

  • Explore university career services and verified internship opportunities.
  • Seek data-related roles in healthcare, biotechnology or general business.
  • Build a portfolio showing the question, dataset, method and findings for each project.
  • Practise aptitude tests, SQL questions, case studies and interview communication.
  • Keep higher studies as an alternative pathway if it supports future goals.
